Join Wall Street Club

We're looking for students who want to build real quant models, write real equity research, and compete — not just attend meetings. No finance background required, just curiosity and follow-through.

Quant Finance Track

Work on systematic strategy research, ML models, and trading infrastructure alongside the quant team.

Equity Research Track

Build valuation models and write sector or single-stock research under the equity research desk.

Events & Competitions

Compete in and help run the Math Olympiad, Poker Championship, and Equity Pitch Night.

Ready to get involved?

Applications open at the start of each semester. Reach out and we'll walk you through the process and current openings.
